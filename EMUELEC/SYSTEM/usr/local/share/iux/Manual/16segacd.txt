Picodrive Core MD-CD Emulator
The universal shortcut key functions are as follows:
Menu: Right side menu key
Exit: SE+ST pressed twice
Quick save: SE+R1 Quick load: SE+L1 Acceleration: SE+R2
Brightness: SE+Arrow keys up/down
Volume: Left side volume/SE+key up/down
Auto-fire: Hold R2 down without releasing, and then press the key you want to turn on auto-fire,
      e.g.2 held down   A is on A key auto-fire, press B is B auto-fire, and to turn off auto-fire just hold R2 the activated autofire key again.
	  
The Picodrive core is a highly compatible MD emulator capable of running Sega 16/32-bit games and MD-CD titles. The SEGA Mega Drive CD features:
Processor: Motorola 68000 (7.67 MHz) + APU Z80A (3.58 MHz)
Memory: M-RAM 72K BYTE, V-RAM 64K BYTE
Graphics: Maximum 512 colors, resolution up to 320x448
Dimensions: 280x212x70mm
Accessories: Controller, dedicated AC transformer (DIN socket); optional modem and CD-ROM drive.
Launched in the early 1990s during the CD boom, this first Genesis accessory allowed 16-bit full-motion video gaming.
Reasons for failure:
High price of $299.
Released in the late stage of 16-bit gaming.
Offered minimal improvement to gameplay experience.
It sold only 6 million units during its short lifespan. More critically, the Sega CD’s poor product support damaged Sega’s reputation, ultimately contributing to the company’s downfall in the console hardware market.
