PokemonMini Game Emulator
The universal shortcut key functions are as follows:
Menu: Right side menu key
Exit: SE+ST pressed twice
Quick save: SE+R1 Quick load: SE+L1 Acceleration: SE+R2
Brightness: SE+Arrow keys up/down
Volume: Left side volume/SE+key up/down
Auto-fire: Hold R2 down without releasing, and then press the key you want to turn on auto-fire,
      e.g.2 held down   A is on A key auto-fire, press B is B auto-fire, and to turn off auto-fire just hold R2 the activated autofire key again.
	  
Game & Watch (Japanese: ゲーム & ウオッチ) is a famous gaming series by Nintendo. The PokemonMini emulator is for the smallest cartridge-based handheld console ever released by Nintendo in 2001! Despite its poor graphical capabilities, this handheld is extremely functional, featuring shake sensors, a vibration motor, a real-time clock, infrared multiplayer battles, and other rich features. Due to its limited production, many people never had the chance to experience its charm.
