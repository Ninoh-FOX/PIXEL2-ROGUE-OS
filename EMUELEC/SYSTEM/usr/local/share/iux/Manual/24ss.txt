Yabasanshiro Sega Saturn (SS) Emulator Standalone Version
The Yabasanshiro Sega Saturn (SS) emulator standalone version offers fast speed and supports high-definition resolution, though compatibility is poor for some games.
Notes:
If a game fails to launch or experiences screen glitches, try the SS-RA emulator. For Saturn Bomberman, press the Start button after starting the game; cutscenes may cause frame freezing.

Player 2: Configure Player 2's controller.
