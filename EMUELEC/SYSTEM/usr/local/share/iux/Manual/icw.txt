Icewaymusic Player Version 1.5 Update:
Added multi-language support (Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, Spanish).
Added a white cassette tape theme.
Added LR quick switching for album support.
Optimized asynchronous loading of playlist cache—only the first load is slow (about 4 minutes for 200 songs), with fast loading thereafter.
Added a folder module to play songs from custom folders (selecting a song in the folder will automatically switch the playlist to all songs in the current folder).
Fixed the issue with random playback not working.
Fixed unresponsive buttons and lack of alerts when turning on the screen after standby with the screen off.
Fixed misoperations caused by long-held LR refresh buttons not being released.
Made minor detail adjustments and bug fixes.
VIP authentication now supports Wi-Fi network activation—no need to copy keys; 开通 VIP can be done online with Wi-Fi.
