Chinese Paladin (仙剑奇侠传)
Button Instructions:
A: Confirm
B: Menu
X: Repeat Last Spell
Y: Auto-Attack
L1: Flee/Exit
R1: Defend
L2 (Mini): Page Up
R2 (Mini): Page Down
F2 (Mini): Use Item
F1 (Mini): Discard Item
Select: Check Status
Start: Confirm
Cheat Instructions:
Map Cheats: Hold X or Y on the map to move freely through obstacles.
Combat Cheats: Hold A before performing different actions in battle:
Hold A before attacking: Deal 30,000 HP damage.
Hold A before taking damage: Take only 1 HP damage.
Hold A before fleeing: Flee successfully every time.
Hold A before stealing: Steal items guaranteed.
Hidden Story Trigger (Dreamland Plot Conditions):
Complete the event of hiring a doctor for Old Man Ding.
Complete the event of helping the poor girl in Suzhou.
Return the fishing rod.
Help the scholar but only accept 1,000 Wen as reward.
Complete the love letter event in the Capital.
Complete the beggar and prostitute event.
Pay the fortune teller's reward.
Unlocked Plot Elements (after entering Dreamland):
Peach Garden Sword Immortal
Dayu Xirang Immortal Beast
Soul Resurrection
Lin Yueru Revival
Nuwa Trial
Baiyue's Escape
Two final endings: Zhao Linger's sacrifice or the perfect ending (explore independently).
After meeting all conditions, speaking to the fortune teller will trigger changed dialogue and unlock the hidden plot.
Note on Difficulty:
The perfect route has been fully tested. A bug causes some bosses to self-destruct for instant victory (beneficial for saving time). The game is highly challenging—choose to play normally or use cheats based on preference. Enjoy!
Additional Settings:
If the default music volume overwhelms sound effects, edit the sdlpal.cfg file in the game directory and set:
MusicVolume=50
