Game Boy Advance (GBA)
Released by Nintendo on March 21, 2001, the Game Boy Advance (GBA) is the second-generation portable gaming console. Its most notable feature is a color TFT LCD screen. Priced at 9,800 yen, it is backward-compatible with games from the previous-generation Game Boy and Game Boy Color. The GBA was launched after Nintendo's prior release of the Game Boy Color, which featured a color LCD screen. The GBA had two revised models: the GBA SP (released in 2003) and the GBM (released in 2005). By 2010, the GBA had sold a cumulative 81,510,000 units worldwide.
